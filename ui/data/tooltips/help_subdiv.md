<p><b>Control dynamic subdivision detail level</b> for all brushes.</p>
<p><span style='color:#90caf9;'>− / +</span> — decrement or increment the subdivision level by 0.5, enable auto-subdivide, and apply to all brushes<br>
<span style='color:#90caf9;'>Apply All</span> — push cached settings (detail level, auto-subdivide, remove stretching) to every brush at once</p>
<p><span style='color:#81c784;'>&#x2713; Tip:</span> Settings persist across sessions. Use Apply All after changing settings in the LKS settings file.</p>
